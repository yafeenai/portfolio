About

Computer science by discipline, photography by instinct. Turning caffeine into code since 2021.

Off The Clock

Chasing waterfalls whenever the semester allows it.

I'm an Applied Computer Science graduate of Dalhousie University, based in Halifax. These days that means debugging something at 2 a.m. for a job instead of a problem set; most weekends it still means being the person a couple trusts to point a camera at their wedding.

The two habits feed each other more than people expect. Photography is about noticing what's actually in front of you before deciding what to do with it — where the light is falling, who's about to laugh, which angle tells the truth. I bring the same instinct to a data problem: look at what's actually there before reaching for a framework.

Before computer science I spent a term studying business and technology management. I switched because I wanted to build things, not just plan them, and I haven't looked back since. Aerial and astrophotography came later, whenever the sky and a free weekend lined up.
