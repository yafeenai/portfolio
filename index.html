About

I'm Eyafee Al Hossain, a Computer Science graduate from Dalhousie University who picked up a camera somewhere between problem sets and never put it back down. Weddings, concerts and events around Halifax came first; aerial and astrophotography followed whenever the sky cooperated. I still think like an engineer: methodical, detail-obsessed. On a shoot, that just means showing up prepared and staying out of the way until the real moment happens.
